CapstoneMEMS & Sensor Design2026

MEMS exhaled-breath sensor for early lung-cancer screening

Some volatile organic compounds in exhaled breath are linked to lung cancer. I designed a small heated gas sensor that could pick them up, as a step toward cheap, non-invasive screening.

What I did

  • Designed a chemiresistive micro-hotplate sensor with an SnO₂–WO₃ sensing film.
  • Chose a target biomarker panel: acetone, formaldehyde, isoprene and aldehyde derivatives.
  • Laid out the fabrication process flow and the read-out chain from sensor to wireless link.
  • Proposed next steps: ML-based VOC classification and a point-of-care device.

Team mini projectDevices & Digital IC Lab2026

Digital Reflex Arena

A hand-reaction game with no microcontroller and no code, built from ten logic chips. One of four red LEDs lights at random, and the player has under a second to wave a hand over the matching infrared sensor.

How it works

  • Three flip-flops and an XOR gate form a 3-bit LFSR that picks the next target each clock cycle.
  • NAND gates check "target lit and hand present" for each sensor to produce a CORRECT signal.
  • A single XOR gate judges each move: WRONG = ANY XOR CORRECT. A wrong move sounds a buzzer through a BC547 transistor.
  • Hits are counted on three green LEDs in binary.
  • The full wire list was checked in software for opens and shorts, and the game was simulated gate by gate before building.
  • Sequential logic
  • Combinational logic
  • 74HC series
  • FC-51 IR sensors
  • LTspice

EMTI LLT-1 Simulations

Five simulations that test core electromagnetics results numerically, each with plotted results and a written inference.

What I simulated

  • Gauss's law: numerical flux through spheres of growing radius, and what happens when the charge sits outside.
  • Displacement current in a parallel-plate capacitor across frequency and plate area.
  • Attenuation and phase on a lossy transmission line from 10 MHz to 1 GHz.
  • A quarter-wave transformer matching a 100 Ω load to a 50 Ω line, swept across frequency.
  • Smith chart analysis of a 75 + j25 Ω load on a 50 Ω line.
  • Electrostatics
  • Maxwell's equations

PrototypePersonal project2026

RetinaScan

A browser-based screening aid for diabetic retinopathy and glaucoma. You drop in a fundus photograph and it shows the vessels, possible lesions and the optic cup, with every processing step visible.

What it does

  • Runs a five-stage image pipeline, including colour balancing and CLAHE contrast equalisation in CIELab space.
  • Weights detail enhancement by local contrast, so vessels sharpen while flat areas stay quiet.
  • Traces the vessel tree, finds the optic disc, cup and fovea, and flags bright and red lesion candidates.
  • Processes photos entirely on the device; nothing is uploaded.
  • Built for research and education. It does not diagnose.
  • Image processing
  • JavaScript
  • Canvas
  • TensorFlow.js
